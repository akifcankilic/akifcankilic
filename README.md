# Akif Can Kilic

I work around air cargo operations.

Mostly on the parts people notice only when something goes wrong:
pricing details, contract wording, small operational decisions
that quietly turn into money or time losses.

A lot of what I do doesn’t end up public.
Internal tools, ad-hoc automation, checks that run in the background,
things meant to reduce follow-ups, emails, and “can you recheck this?” moments.

---

## What I deal with

- Ground handling processes in air cargo  
- SGHA contracts, Annex B items, what is *actually* included vs not  
- Billing checks, tariff logic, revenue leakage  
- CPI / LCI adjustments and retroactive corrections  
- Automation where manual judgment keeps repeating  

---

## Approach

I usually start by reading how things are *really* done,
not how they are written in procedures.

If a rule is enough, I keep it simple.  
If judgment is needed, I automate the boring part first.

Tools come last.
